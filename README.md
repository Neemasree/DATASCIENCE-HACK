# 📦 StockSense — AI-Powered Inventory Intelligence Platform

> **Predict demand. Detect stock-out risk. Recommend smarter replenishment.**

StockSense is an AI-powered inventory intelligence platform designed to help retailers make better inventory decisions using machine learning, demand forecasting, stock-out prediction, and actionable recommendations.

The system analyzes historical sales, inventory, store information, product information, and external factors to generate:

- 📈 7-day demand forecasts
- 🚨 Stock-out risk predictions
- 🟢🟡🔴 Risk classification
- 📦 Replenishment recommendations
- 💰 Revenue-at-risk insights
- 🏪 Store and category-level analytics
- 🧠 Explainable business insights
- 🎯 Manager action recommendations

---

## 🎯 Problem

Retailers need to maintain the right amount of inventory across multiple stores and products.

Too little inventory can result in:

- Stock-outs
- Lost sales
- Poor customer experience
- Emergency replenishment

Too much inventory can result in:

- Excess holding costs
- Overstock
- Capital being locked in inventory
- Reduced inventory efficiency

Traditional inventory systems often show **what is happening**, but do not clearly answer:

> **"What is likely to happen next, and what should the manager do about it?"**

StockSense addresses this gap by combining machine learning with an action-oriented inventory dashboard.
